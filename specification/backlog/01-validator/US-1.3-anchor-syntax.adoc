[[US-1.3]]
=== US-1.3 Anchor syntax that survives rendering

Status:: Done (0.1.0) +
Effort:: 2.5 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-1.1>>, <<TS-1.4>>, <<FR-1.2>>, <<FR-1.7>>

As an author of a specification, I want the validator to reject `+[#FR-1.2]+` and to rewrite it
for me, so that every item keeps its own id in the rendered HTML.

.Acceptance criteria
. `+[[ID]]+` is the anchor; `+[#ID]+` is reported with the message of FR-1.2 and still resolves
  references.
. `-fix` rewrites all shorthand anchors, keeps CRLF and trailing blanks, lists the changed
  files and then validates.
. This repository's specification is migrated with `-fix`.

.Test criteria
* Unit test: a shorthand anchor gives exactly one problem; `FixAnchors` on LF and CRLF files,
  followed by a clean validation.
* Component test: `-fix` on a project with a shorthand anchor.
