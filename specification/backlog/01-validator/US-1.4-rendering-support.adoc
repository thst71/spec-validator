[[US-1.4]]
=== US-1.4 Links between the rendered masters

Status:: Done (0.1.0) +
Effort:: 2 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-1.3>>, <<TS-1.4>>, <<FR-1.8>>

As the release pipeline, I want the list of masters and the references across masters turned
into links, so that each master becomes one HTML file and the files link each other.

.Acceptance criteria
. `-masters` prints the masters in configuration order.
. `-link <dir>` rewrites references to other masters in the flattened masters as
  inter-document xrefs and keeps references within a master.
. `-link` writes nothing for an invalid specification.
. On wos-intel, `-fix`, `asciidoctor-reducer`, `-link` and `asciidoctor --failure-level INFO`
  render the four masters without unresolved references (except the literal `+<<ID>>+` in
  its TS-4.2, which wos-intel fixes).

.Test criteria
* Unit test: `Link` on a tree with references within and across masters; refusal on an invalid
  tree; `relativeMaster` for nested master paths.
* Component test: `-masters`, `-link`, `-link` with problems, mutually exclusive modes.
* Integration test (manual, 0.1.0): the render chain on a copy of wos-intel in the
  `asciidoctor/docker-asciidoctor:1.108.0` image.
