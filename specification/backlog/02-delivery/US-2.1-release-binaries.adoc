=== US-2.1 Release binaries and quality gate

Status:: Done (0.1.0) +
Effort:: 3 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-2.1>>, <<FR-2.1>>, <<NFR-2>>

As a maintainer of the agent-skills library, I want checksummed release binaries for every
platform, so that the library's launcher can pin and verify them.

.Acceptance criteria
. `task check` passes locally and in CI.
. Pushing the tag `v0.1.0` publishes the six archives and `checksums.txt`.
. The downloaded darwin/arm64 binary reports `0.1.0` with `-version`.

.Test criteria
* Blackbox test (manual, per release): download an archive, verify its checksum, run
  `-version` and validate wos-intel.
