[[US-3.4]]
=== US-3.4 Rendered reports

Status:: Done (0.2.0) +
Effort:: 2 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-3.5>>, <<FR-3.6>>

As the release pipeline, I want the reports rendered like the other masters, so that the
release documentation links requirements, scenarios and results.

.Acceptance criteria
. `-masters` lists existing report masters after the configured ones.
. `-link` rewrites references between the reports and the other masters.
. The render chain of release-actions (`asciidoctor-reducer`, `-link`, `asciidoctor
  --failure-level INFO`) renders a fixture with both reports and a story referencing an AT.

.Technical tasks
. `Masters` and `analyze` know the report masters.
. Tests below.

.Test criteria
* Unit test: `Masters` with and without report files; `Link` with references from the
  requirements to `AT` and from the reports to FR.
* Component test: `-masters` on the fixture.
* Integration test: the render chain on the fixture in Docker (skipped without Docker).
* Blackbox test: none; see <<US-3.1>>.
