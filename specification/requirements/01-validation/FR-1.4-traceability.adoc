=== FR-1.4 Traceability

.Acceptance criteria
. A traceability rule requires every item of a kind to reference at least one item of a set of
  kinds within its section (the text up to the next anchor).
. A violation is reported as `<file>: <ID> references no <name>`, with the rule's name.
. Default rules: TS → FR or NFR ("requirement"); US → FR or NFR ("requirement");
  US → TS ("technical specification").
