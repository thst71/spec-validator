=== FR-3.3 Scenario traces

.Acceptance criteria
. A scenario traces to the items named by its tags and the tags of its `Feature` and `Rule`
  (inherited), e.g. `@FR-2.1 @NFR-3`.
. A trace tag must resolve to an anchor: `<file>:<line>: AT-2.1 traces to unknown FR-9.9`.
. A trace tag of an anchor kind that the test kind does not trace to is reported:
  `<file>:<line>: AT-2.1 traces to US-1.2; AT traces to FR, NFR only`.
. A scenario with no trace to a requirement is reported: `<file>:<line>: AT-2.1 references no
  requirement`.
. Other tags (e.g. `@wip`, `@slow`) are allowed.
