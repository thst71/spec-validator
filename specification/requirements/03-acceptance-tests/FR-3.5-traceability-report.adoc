=== FR-3.5 Traceability report

.Acceptance criteria
. `-trace-report` writes the traceability report master (FR-3.1) in the project root.
. Top-down: every FR and NFR with the scenarios covering it; with `-results <results.json>`,
  each with its result (FR-3.4).
. Bottom-up: every scenario with the requirements it traces to.
. Orphaned scenarios (none of whose traces resolves to a requirement) and uncovered
  requirements (no scenario traces to them) are listed in their own sections; an empty
  section says "none".
. A summary gives the number of requirements, covered requirements and orphaned scenarios.
. The report is written even when it finds orphaned scenarios or uncovered requirements; the
  exit code is 0. A rule that fails on them is left to a later version.
