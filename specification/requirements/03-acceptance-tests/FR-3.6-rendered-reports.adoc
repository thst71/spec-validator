=== FR-3.6 Rendered reports

.Acceptance criteria
. `-masters` also prints the report masters that exist in the project root, after the
  configured masters.
. `-link` turns references between the reports and the other masters into inter-document
  xrefs: from the reports to requirements, from the traceability report to the scenarios of
  the test report, and from any master to `AT` IDs when the test report exists.
. Without report files, `-masters` and `-link` behave as before.
